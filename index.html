✕ Microphone Troubleshooting Tips:
1. Click the Lock icon (🔒) right next to your browser’s web link bar at the top and ensure the Microphone option is switched to Allow.
2. Ensure your Mac system settings permit microphone access under System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Note: Microphone features stay disabled inside the internal Rise edit layout screen. Use the Preview mode to test functions!
